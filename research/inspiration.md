# Inspiration: personal sites with real personality

Checked live in a browser on 2026-10-25. Screenshots: `.playwright-mcp/insp-*.png`.

## The one lesson

None of these is memorable because of animation or gradients. Each has at least one thing only that
person could have put there: their face, their hobby, their voice, their own invention. The v2 and v3
attempts had none of that, which is why they read as AI-made.

## The sites, and what to take

| Site | What makes it theirs | What we could take |
|---|---|---|
| [Lynn Fisher](https://lynnandtonic.com) | Redesigns every year (now v.XIX); this year it's an old book's contents page in red on black. | A **single strong metaphor** carried all the way through, instead of a "portfolio layout". |
| [Bruno Simon](https://bruno-simon.com) | You drive a toy car through a 3D world to find his work. The site *is* the skill. | The site should **demonstrate** what you do, not describe it. |
| [Bartosz Ciechanowski](https://ciechanow.ski) | Plain blog chrome, but every post is a hand-built interactive you drag and scrub. | **Let visitors play with the idea** (an agent, an eval, a memory layer) instead of reading about it. |
| [Maggie Appleton](https://maggieappleton.com) | "Designer, anthropologist, and mediocre developer". A self-aware voice, plus hand-drawn illustrations. | **Voice.** Write like a person, with a joke or an admission in it. |
| [Robb Owen](https://robbowen.digital) | An illustrated self-portrait that animates, and a line in Welsh in the margin. | **Your face and your place**, drawn. |
| [Cassidy Williams](https://cassidoo.co) | A photo of her holding up a mechanical keyboard; "memes and dreams and software". | **One real photo** that says what you're into. |
| [Jhey Tompkins](https://jhey.dev) | Signature, a claw machine hanging from the top, and a live footer: "eatin' roasties in Bedford", weather, now playing. | **Live, tiny, true details** (what you're building or listening to right now). |
| [Rauno Freiberg](https://rauno.me) | One sentence about himself, set huge, with one yellow circle. Every interaction is fussed over. | **Restraint**: one idea, executed perfectly. |
| [Emil Kowalski](https://emilkowal.ski) / [Paco Coursey](https://paco.me) | Almost plain text. The craft is in the details and the writing. | Proof that **plain can still be personal** if the words are good. |
| [Dennis Snellenberg](https://dennissnellenberg.com) | Big photo of him, his name scrolling huge across it. Agency polish. | **A real photo, used big.** (Also a warning: this style is widely copied.) |
| [Neal.fun](https://neal.fun) | Not a portfolio, a toybox of hand-illustrated games. | **Small playable toys** as the projects themselves. |
| [Josh Comeau](https://www.joshwcomeau.com) | A 3D model of himself on a rainbow, sound effects, whimsy everywhere. | **Whimsy and sound**, used sparingly. |

## Four directions these suggest for Rohan

1. **Play with it.** (Bruno, Ciechanowski, Neal) The page is a small working toy built from your
   real work, e.g. drop a messy "seller document" in and watch a toy agent check it, retry, and pass
   it. You show you build agents by handing visitors one.
2. **One metaphor, all the way.** (Lynn Fisher) Pick one object and commit to it: a terminal session,
   a lab notebook, a train ticket from Delhi through Dubai, Purdue and New York.
3. **Your voice, plain and sharp.** (Maggie, Paco, Emil, Cassidy) Mostly text, beautifully set, with
   a real photo and a few live details. Cheap to build; lives or dies on the writing.
4. **Your face and place, illustrated.** (Robb, Josh, Jhey) A drawn or 3D you, Delhi in it, small
   interactive moments.

## What we need from Rohan (none of this can be invented)

- A real photo, or permission for an illustrated portrait.
- Two or three things you're into outside work.
- How you'd describe yourself in one honest, slightly funny sentence.
- One project you'd love a visitor to actually *play with*.
